# Science Calculators

Science calculators from [CalciHub](https://calcihub.com/) covering common physics and chemistry equations and relationships.

The calculators are designed to show the relevant equation, define its variables, and make the calculation easier to reproduce.

## What this repository contains

The Science collection currently includes **8 calculators** covering:

- Electricity
- Density
- Radioactive decay
- Waves
- Kinetic energy
- Force
- Gravitation
- Molar mass

See the [Science Calculator Index](calculators/README.md) for the complete list.

## Calculation methodology

Each calculator uses a defined scientific relationship or equation.

Where relevant, documentation explains:

- The equation used
- Definitions of variables
- Units
- Assumptions
- How to interpret the result
- Important limitations

These calculators generally represent idealized relationships and may not account for every condition present in real-world experiments or systems.
